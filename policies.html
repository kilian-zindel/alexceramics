Policies

The short version: I ship within the US for a flat $12, all sales are final, and if a piece arrives broken I'll refund you in full. The details are below. By buying from the store you agree to them.

Shipping · Returns & refunds · Privacy · Contact

Shipping

I ship within the United States only. Shipping is a flat $12 per order, shown at checkout before you pay.

Your piece ships within 5 business days of your order, packed in lots of bubble wrap. Delivery time after that depends on the carrier and where you are.

If something holds me up and I can't ship in that time, I'll email you with a new date, and you can cancel for a full refund if you'd rather not wait.

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email me right away and I'll fix it if the piece hasn't shipped yet.

Changed your mind? If your piece hasn't shipped yet, email me and I'll cancel your order and refund you in full. Once it's shipped, the sale is final (see below).

Returns & refunds

All sales are final. I don't accept returns or exchanges. Every piece is one of a kind and handmade, so small marks, glaze variations and slight differences from the photos are part of it, not defects.

If your piece arrives broken or damaged, you get a full refund, including shipping. Here's how:

  • Email me within 7 days of delivery at alexkoe2001@gmail.com.
  • Include your name and a few photos of the damage and of the box and packaging.
  • Please don't send the piece back. You don't need to.

If your package gets lost (it never shows up), email me and I'll check with the carrier. If it's lost, I'll refund you in full, including shipping.

Because each piece is one of a kind, I can't send a replacement. Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe, usually within 2 business days of your email. Your bank may take another 5–10 business days to show it. There are no fees, and refunds are always money back, not store credit.

You're welcome to ask me for a written copy of this policy at any time.

Privacy

I only collect what I need to get your piece to you.

  • What I get: when you buy something, the Stripe checkout asks for your name, email, shipping address and phone number. That's what I see. Your card details go straight to Stripe; I never see or store them. If you email or message me, I'll have whatever you send.
  • How I use it: to ship your order, contact you about your order, handle refunds, and keep the sales records I'm required to keep for taxes. No newsletter, no marketing emails, no ads.
  • Who else sees it: Stripe processes payments and sends your receipt. The shipping carrier gets your name and address (and phone number if they need it for delivery). That's it. I never sell or share your information with anyone else.
  • This website: it has no accounts, no analytics and no cookies of its own. It's hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (like IP addresses), and it loads fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when the page loads. Stripe's checkout page may use its own cookies to prevent fraud. Since I don't track you, there's nothing for a browser "Do Not Track" setting to turn off.
  • Your choices: email me if you'd like to see, correct or delete the information I have about you. I'll do it, except for records I have to keep for taxes.

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the bottom of the page.
